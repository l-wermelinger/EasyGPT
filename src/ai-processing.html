The AI Processing Pipeline

Understanding how AI transforms your input into meaningful responses

📝

Input Tokenization

Your text is broken down into smaller units called tokens - words, subwords, or characters that the AI can process mathematically.

🔢

Numerical Encoding

Each token is converted into numerical vectors (embeddings) that represent semantic meaning in high-dimensional space.

🧠

Attention Mechanism

The AI calculates attention weights to determine which parts of the input are most relevant for generating the response.

⚡

Parallel Processing

Multiple layers of neural networks process the information simultaneously, each extracting different patterns and features.

🎯

Response Generation

Based on learned patterns, the AI predicts the most likely next tokens to generate a coherent and relevant response.
